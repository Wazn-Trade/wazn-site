01 — Why

A Sharia verdict should be auditable.

Most compliance lists give you a yes or a no without saying why, when it was last updated, or against which standard. We show you the arithmetic.

Every verdict shows its reasons

The ratios, the limits, the page in the financial statements and the standard applied. No "trust us".

Correct on any date

point-in-time data, refreshed with every semi-annual EGX33 review and every quarterly result. Ask: was this stock compliant on a given day?

Built for agents

A REST API for your app, and an MCP server that lets Claude or any AI agent screen your portfolio from the conversation.

02 — How it works

Three layers. A stock has to pass all of them.

Any doubt in the data rejects the stock. When we are not sure, we fail closed; nothing passes by default.

  1. EGX33 Shariah membership

    The stock must be in the index review in force on the date you ask about. If it drops out, it is marked Excluded immediately.

  2. AAOIFI ratios from the statements

    Interest-bearing debt, interest-bearing cash and non-compliant income, from the latest published statements. Close to a limit means Watchlist, before the next review.

  3. Verdict and purification

    Compliant, Watchlist or Excluded, with the reasons, plus the purification ratio to apply to dividends.

06 — FAQ

Before you ask.

Is a result a fatwa?

No. Wazn Screen applies the EGX33 Shariah index methodology and AAOIFI Standard No. 21 to published data and shows the arithmetic. Contested questions belong to qualified scholars.

Do you give buy or sell recommendations?

No. Wazn Screen is a data and screening tool. It never tells you what to buy and has no trading permissions.

Where does the data come from?

EGX33 membership from the Egyptian Exchange's semi-annual reviews; ratios from companies' published statements and disclosures. Every response cites its source and date.

Is it open source?

The screening layer will be published on github.com/Wazn-Trade so anyone can audit the logic.
